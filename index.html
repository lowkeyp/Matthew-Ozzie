MATTHEW OZZIE

There isn't really much to explain.
I'm simply here, taking things as they come,
finding meaning in some moments and letting others pass.

Nothing here is meant to tell a complete story.
Some things are better left unsaid, some memories are better
kept quietly, and some moments are simply meant to be lived.

Maybe life doesn't always need a reason or a direction.
Sometimes, it's enough to just be present and see where things go.
